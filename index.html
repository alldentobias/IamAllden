§ 02 — About

About Tobias

Senior engineer. Backend by trade, machine learning by appetite, agentic commerce by current obsession.

Tobias Alldén is a senior software engineer based in Oslo, Norway, working on the Agentic Commerce team at Vipps MobilePay, where he helps merchants prepare for a checkout era driven by AI agents. He previously led backend and infrastructure for Aion Loop at AION, a greentech platform for plastic-waste traceability, and spent nearly four years at Microsoft as a senior software engineer building recommender systems and MLOps for the Microsoft feed, which served more than 140 million monthly active users. He writes about agentic commerce, recommender systems, and applied AI at allden.dev.

§ 05 — Questions

Frequently asked

What does Tobias Alldén do?

Tobias Alldén is a senior software engineer at Vipps MobilePay in Oslo, working on the Agentic Commerce team. His focus is helping Nordic merchants accept payments and run checkout flows that are initiated and completed by AI agents on behalf of consumers. He has nine years of professional software-engineering experience, primarily in backend systems and applied machine learning.

What is agentic commerce, and how does Tobias Alldén work on it?

Agentic commerce is the emerging category of e-commerce in which AI assistants act on a shopper's behalf — discovering products, comparing options, and completing checkout. At Vipps MobilePay, Tobias Alldén works on the engineering surface that lets merchants accept payments from these agents, including support for the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) and the Vipps MobilePay Payment Handler.

What did Tobias Alldén build at Microsoft?

From August 2019 to January 2023, Tobias Alldén was a senior software engineer at Microsoft working on the Microsoft feed, the personalized content surface that reaches more than 140 million monthly active users. His work covered recommender-system backends and MLOps — the infrastructure that takes machine-learning models from notebook to production at consumer scale.

What did Tobias Alldén do at AION?

At AION (January 2023 to February 2024), Tobias Alldén was the lead backend and infrastructure engineer on Aion Loop, a greentech traceability platform for plastic waste. He built and operated the systems that tracked plastic flows across recycling supply chains.
